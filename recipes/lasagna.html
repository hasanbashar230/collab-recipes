Lasagna

Description

A classic Italian baked pasta dish with layers of meat sauce, béchamel and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500g minced beef
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 300ml béchamel sauce
  • 200g mozzarella

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook meat sauce and simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Layer pasta, sauce and béchamel in a dish.
  4. Top with mozzarella and bake for 35 minutes.
